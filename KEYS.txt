FNaE Native test controls (desktop)

Enter  = start / advance / retry
ESC    = quit
S      = camera up/down
1-4    = camera selection
A      = left door
D      = right door
M      = mask
Z      = flashlight
E      = audio lure (camera up, not Cam 04)
R      = wind music box (hold, Cam 04)
Click door wall buttons (51x56, beside each door) = toggle that door
Click desk flip zone (x 500-780, y > 560) = camera up

Wii U GamePad (hardware only; cameras live on the GamePad screen, the
office and everything else stays on the TV)

ZL        = cameras open/close
L / R     = left / right door
Y (hold)  = wind music box (Cam 04)
B (hold)  = flashlight (back to title on the customize screen)
X         = audio lure (camera up, not Cam 04)
ZR        = mask
A / Plus  = start / advance / retry (Enter)
Minus     = mute phone call
D-pad Up/Down    = menu navigation
D-pad Left/Right = switch camera (camera up) / menus otherwise
Left stick       = look around the office (pan)
Touchscreen      = camera buttons and lure (tap)
